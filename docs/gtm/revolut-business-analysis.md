## Evidence Boundaries

- Do not reuse headline savings or speed claims as guaranteed outcomes.
- Do not assume every feature is included in the entry-level plan.
- Verify UK eligibility, contractual entity and protection arrangements
  separately before making related claims.
- The portfolio simulation does not reproduce the provider's full product
  or establish its actual architecture or security controls.
