# Hypothetical 90-Day Acquisition Pilot

**Prepared:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** Planning exercise; not launched
**Audience:** Provisional UK limited-company agency ICP
**Timing:** Relative days; no launch date scheduled

## Objective

Test whether the selected audience has a meaningful cross-border
payment or administration problem, can be reached through the proposed
channels and voluntarily progresses to a suitable product evaluation.

Acquisition outcomes are hypotheses. No customers, applications,
approvals or activated accounts are claimed.

## Days 1-30: Validate the Problem

Proposed work:
- Recheck material pricing, product and eligibility sources.
- Resolve representative cross-border costs where possible.
- Verify essential integration and permission requirements.
- Prepare channel attribution and discovery records.
- Conduct voluntary discovery only if separately authorised.

Decision gate:
Proceed only if discovery identifies a recurring, meaningful problem
and a feasible operational use case. Record contradictory evidence.

## Days 31-60: Test Access and Messaging

Proposed work:
- Test the selected channels within an agreed resource budget.
- Use consistent qualification and funnel definitions.
- Compare message relevance across decision-maker roles.
- Record staff time, direct expenditure and disqualification reasons.
- Offer a documented comparison where requirements can be assessed.

Decision gate:
Continue only if relevant conversations progress to qualified interest
at a cost that can be evaluated against the commercial model.

## Days 61-90: Evaluate Outcomes

Proposed work:
- Track voluntary evaluation decisions and known application outcomes.
- Keep applications, approvals and activation separate.
- Gather feedback on suitability and remaining obstacles.
- Review channel costs, conversion assumptions and pending outcomes.
- Recommend continuation, revision or closure.

Decision gate:
Do not scale based on engagement alone. Assess qualified demand,
operational fit and economics using observed evidence.

## Responsibilities in a Hypothetical Execution

| Responsibility | Proposed owner |
|---|---|
| Research, source checks and independent materials | Pilot lead |
| Discovery and qualification records | Pilot lead |
| Comparison of required workflows and costs | Research analyst |
| Account application and product decisions | Applicant and provider |
| Budget and continuation decisions | Hypothetical project sponsor |

Roles describe a proposed operating model. No team appointments,
provider access or sponsorship arrangements exist.

## Budget and Metrics

The next stage will define illustrative resource costs, funnel
assumptions and decision thresholds.

Keep cash expenditure separate from staff-time valuation.
Do not assume referral income or access to provider unit economics.

## Stop or Revise Conditions

- Relevant buyers cannot be reached within the agreed resources.
- Discovery does not establish a meaningful problem.
- Eligibility or essential functionality does not fit.
- Material comparison gaps prevent a fair evaluation.
- Observed costs or progression fail the agreed decision thresholds.
- Participants decline further contact.

## Execution Boundary

This is an independent educational plan. No outreach, publication,
advertising expenditure, applications or commercial agreements have
been undertaken or authorised through this document.
