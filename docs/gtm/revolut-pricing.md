## Observed Monthly Allowances

| Plan | Local transfers | International transfers | Currency exchange |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Basic | 10 | 0 | GBP 1000 |
| Grow | 100 | 5 | GBP 15000 |
| Scale | 1000 | 25 | GBP 60000 |

S008-S010 list local-transfer overage at GBP 0.20 per transfer and
international-transfer overage at GBP 5. FX overage is 0.6%, with an
additional 1% outside FX market hours.

Revolut-to-Revolut transfers have a separate unlimited allowance.
Local-transfer classifications include GBP, EUR and CHF in these schedules.

## Additional Costs and Conditions

- S007 lists expense management at GBP 8 per person per month.
- SWIFT OUR, incoming Fedwire, outbound ACH, card transfers, acquiring,
  card delivery and ATM use can carry separate charges.
- Do not treat subscription plus standard transfer fees as a complete
  cost estimate when those services are used.
- Tax treatment and any promotional conditions remain to be checked.
- Monthly allowances must not be treated as annual pooled allowances.

## Discrepancies and Applicability

- S009 explicitly names Revolut Bank UAB. Confirm which terms apply to
  the intended UK customer and whether migration affects applicability.
- S007 shows a Basic ATM limit different from S008. Do not resolve this
  by assumption; exclude ATM scenarios until the applicable limit is verified.
- Marketing starting prices and monthly-billed prices use different bases.
- Treat these figures as observed evidence, not final approved model inputs.
