## Executive Decision

Select UK limited-company agencies with 1-49 employees and recurring
overseas earned revenue as the provisional audience for a hypothetical
90-day discovery and acquisition pilot.

This is a working research choice, not a validated market opportunity.
Agencies score 3.80/5 in the assumption-based framework, compared with
3.45 for technology services and 3.05 for international e-commerce.
Reducing two agency assumptions by one point removes its lead.

Evaluate Revolut Business where its advertised payment, currency and
administrative capabilities fit the buyer's actual requirements.
Compare relevant alternatives and complete costs before recommending
further evaluation.

## Commercial Question

Can this audience be reached economically, demonstrate a meaningful
payment or administration problem and voluntarily progress to a
suitable product evaluation?

The available research does not establish the answer. The proposed
pilot specifies how to investigate it.

## Research Findings

- Broad UK business statistics provide market context, not a verified
  count of eligible or reachable agencies.
- Official product pages establish advertised capabilities, subject
  to plan, eligibility and operational checks.
- The defined domestic scenario does not support a lowest-cost claim
  for Revolut.
- Comparable cross-border quotes remain unresolved.
- Buyer access, pain intensity and willingness to evaluate remain
  assumptions requiring customer validation.
- The portfolio's technical implementation does not establish the
  provider's actual architecture or controls.

Source identifiers, dates and limitations are recorded in
[sources.csv](sources.csv). Findings must be read with their supporting
documents rather than treated as universal product conclusions.

## Proposed Strategy

| Decision | Proposal | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Target | UK limited-company agencies with recurring cross-border activity | Individual eligibility and activity checks required |
| Buyer | Founder, finance or operations decision-maker | Establish actual authority through discovery |
| Positioning | Evaluate payment and administration fit against complete costs | No guaranteed savings or unique-feature claim |
| Channels | LinkedIn content, agency communities and adviser introductions | Access and conversion are unvalidated |
| Pilot | Three phases over 90 relative days | No launch scheduled or execution authorised |
| Measurement | Unique businesses, qualified interest, evaluations and activation | Keep funnel stages and pending outcomes separate |

## Illustrative Economics

The model assumes 75 staff hours valued at GBP30 per hour, plus GBP150
direct cash: GBP2,400 total economic cost.

Its base funnel assumes:
40 enquiries -> 20 attended discoveries -> 10 qualified businesses ->
4 evaluations -> 2 expected activations.

This produces GBP75 cash cost and GBP1,200 economic cost per expected
activation. These are hypothetical pilot figures, not observed results
or the provider's actual customer acquisition cost.

The illustrative annual gross subscription calculation is not profit,
90-day revenue or income to SkynetAI-Labs. No referral agreement or
commission is assumed. Provider profitability remains unresolved.

See [commercial-model.md](commercial-model.md) for assumptions,
sensitivity, contribution-based break-even and decision thresholds.
