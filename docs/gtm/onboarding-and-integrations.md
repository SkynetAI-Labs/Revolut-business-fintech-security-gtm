# Onboarding and Integration Comparison

**Research date:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** Desk research; no applications or integration trials conducted

## Onboarding

| Provider | Evidence established | Qualification |
|---|---|---|
| Revolut Business | Business, identity, activity and ownership checks described in S011-S014 | Confirm legal form, sector, locations and contractual entity |
| Wise Business | Business details and possible additional documents required, S024 | Requirements depend on the business and associated people |
| Starling Business | Case-by-case approval; company structure, residency and business-activity restrictions, S020 | A UK registered company is not automatically eligible |
| HSBC Small Business Banking | Application requests company details, estimated turnover and director/partner information, S021 | Eligibility, checks and additional information apply |

No provider is assigned a guaranteed onboarding time or approval rate.
Do not use marketing speed claims as a promise to prospects.

## Accounting and Administration

| Provider | Evidence established | Remaining validation |
|---|---|---|
| Revolut Business | Advertised accounting integrations; some controls and API access depend on plan, S006-S007 | Verify bank feeds, expense export, payment functions and required software subscription |
| Wise Business | Direct or open-banking accounting connections, S025 | Verify supported software, currencies, synchronisation and permissions |
| Starling Business | Advertises Xero, QuickBooks and FreeAgent payment synchronisation, S020 | Verify the customer's required workflow and software plan |
| HSBC Small Business Banking | Advertises connected-account visibility; Sage-linked offer described for eligible sole traders, S021 | Do not assume the sole-trader offer applies to our limited-company scenario; establish required accounting feed separately |

## Discovery Checklist

1. What is the exact legal form, ownership structure and trading activity?
2. Where are directors, owners, operations and counterparties located?
3. Are receipts earned revenue or money held on behalf of clients?
4. Which accounting product, subscription and currencies are used?
5. Is a transaction feed sufficient, or are payment initiation,
   expense exports and approval workflows required?
6. Who needs access, with which permissions?
7. Which existing banking services must remain available?

## Commercial Implication

Treat onboarding eligibility and operational compatibility as qualification
gates. A named integration is not proof that the buyer's full workflow
is supported.

Record unresolved functions before recommending a plan. No migration,
integration performance or customer outcome has been tested.
