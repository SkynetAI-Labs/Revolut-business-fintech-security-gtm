# UK Business Market — Initial Baseline

**Research accessed:** 23 October 2026

## Verified Facts

- S001 estimates approximately 5.7 million UK private-sector businesses
  at the start of 2025, including estimated unregistered businesses.
- S001 reports 5.64 million businesses with 0-49 employees.
- S002 reports approximately 2.79 million VAT/PAYE-registered businesses
  in March 2026.
- S002 identifies professional, scientific and technical activities as
  15.3% of registered businesses.

## Interpretation

These sources describe different populations and periods. Their totals
must not be added together or treated as a growth comparison.

The professional-services share provides broad sector context only.
It does not establish demand for Revolut Business.

## Employee-Size Refinement

S001 Table C, start of 2025:

| Employees | Published business count |
|---|---:|
| 1-9 | 1,150,875 |
| 10-49 | 220,085 |
| Derived 1-49 total | 1,370,960 |

Calculation: 1,150,875 + 220,085 = 1,370,960.

The sum uses published rounded counts across all industries. It is not
a count of cross-border businesses, eligible applicants or reachable
prospects. DBT classifies some working-proprietor companies as
non-employers, so these are statistical size bands.

S003 schedules the 2026 edition provisionally for 5 November 2026.
The 2025 edition is therefore retained as the available DBT baseline
on 23 October 2026. Recheck availability before final publication.

The earlier employee-count and release-availability questions are now
resolved for this research date. Industry definitions and cross-border
demand remain to be investigated.
