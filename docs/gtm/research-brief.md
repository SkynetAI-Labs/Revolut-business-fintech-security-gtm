# Lab 5 — Research Brief

**Project:** Independent Revolut Business market intelligence and GTM case study
**Author:** SkynetAI-Labs
**Started:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** Research brief in development

## Commercial Questions

1. Which UK SME segment offers the strongest initial opportunity?
2. What banking problems and buying triggers matter to that segment?
3. How does Revolut Business compare with relevant alternatives?
4. What positioning, acquisition channels and sales approach fit the target buyer?
5. What would a measurable 90-day acquisition pilot look like?
6. Under which assumptions could that pilot be commercially viable?

## Relationship to the Technical Project

Labs 1–4 demonstrate a fictional banking API, authentication,
container security and rule-based transaction monitoring.

These implementations provide technical learning context. They do not
establish Revolut's actual architecture, controls or product capabilities.

## Independence

Educational portfolio research only. Not affiliated with or endorsed by
Revolut. No customer outreach, advertising expenditure or external
publication is authorised by this brief.

## Market Scope

- Geography: United Kingdom.
- Audience: SMEs, with an initial analytical focus on businesses
  employing 1–49 people. This is a research focus, not a claim about
  Revolut Business eligibility.
- Product scope: business accounts, domestic and international payments,
  foreign exchange, expense controls and relevant integrations.
- Commercial objective: recommend one primary target segment and a
  hypothetical 90-day acquisition pilot.
- Research date: record the access date for each source. Retain the
  publication date and reporting period where available.

### Candidate Segments

1. Professional-services agencies serving overseas clients.
2. E-commerce businesses with international suppliers or customers.
3. Small software and technology-service businesses with cross-border
   revenue or operating expenditure.

Compare needs, eligibility, competitive alternatives, buyer access and
commercial attractiveness before selecting the primary segment.

### Boundaries

- Do not assume every candidate business is eligible.
- Separate verified product facts from analysis and model assumptions.
- Do not infer actual Revolut capabilities from the portfolio simulation.
- Exclude personal banking, enterprise procurement and credit underwriting.
- Do not claim research interviews, customer validation or campaign
  results unless actually conducted and evidenced.
- Outreach materials and financial projections remain hypothetical.
