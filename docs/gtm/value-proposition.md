# Proposed Value Proposition

**Research date:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** Analyst hypothesis; no measured customer outcomes
**Audience:** Provisional UK limited-company agency ICP

## Core Proposition

Evaluate whether managing international receipts, currency conversion,
supplier payments and relevant administration through Revolut Business
would improve the agency's workflow enough to justify the complete cost.

## Value Hypotheses

| Customer job | Proposed benefit to test | Evidence and condition | Measurement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Receive and convert overseas revenue | Better visibility of conversion and receipt costs | Advertised currency functionality; verify actual routes, rates and charges | Total GBP-equivalent cost for the same receipt and conversion |
| Pay overseas contractors | A suitable payment workflow for recurring obligations | Advertised international transfers; check destination, rail and fees | Total cost and observed completion time for comparable payments |
| Reconcile transactions | Less manual work where the integration fits | Named accounting integrations do not establish full workflow support | Measured reconciliation minutes and unresolved items |
| Delegate financial tasks | Appropriate access and approvals | Check exact permissions and applicable plan | Required controls supported and exceptions recorded |

These are proposed benefits to investigate. None has been demonstrated
through this portfolio exercise.

## Cost and Effort to Include

- Subscription and transaction charges.
- Conversion charges and exchange-rate differences.
- Required software or optional-service costs.
- Setup, onboarding and training effort.
- Additional reconciliation if retaining another account.
- Dependencies or services that the proposed arrangement cannot replace.

## Evaluation Method

1. Record the current workflow and a representative usage baseline.
2. Establish eligibility and required functionality.
3. Compare equivalent payment routes, timing and recipient amounts.
4. Separate one-off costs from recurring costs.
5. Measure operational outcomes only through an authorised trial.
6. Report negative or inconclusive results alongside positive findings.

## Decision Rule

Recommend further evaluation only when the required functions are
supported and the buyer can assess the complete costs and trade-offs.

Do not convert an assumed time saving into financial value without
measuring the time change and stating the valuation assumptions.

## Evidence Limits

No guaranteed savings, faster payments, reduced administration or
improved controls are claimed. No customer trial has been conducted.
This is independent educational analysis, not an official provider offer.
