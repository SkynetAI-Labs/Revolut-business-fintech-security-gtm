# Eligibility, Onboarding and Terms

**Accessed:** 23 October 2026
**Sources:** S011-S015

## Contractual Entity

S011 names Revolut Bank UK Ltd. S014 describes migration from Revolut
Ltd e-money accounts. Do not assume all existing accounts have migrated.

Protection claims must identify the account entity and applicable
eligibility and exclusions. Do not equate safeguarding with deposit
insurance. Independent regulator/scheme verification remains outstanding.

## Qualification Focus

For this case study, initially qualify UK private limited companies.
This is an analyst scope choice, not approval by Revolut.

S012 also lists LLP, LP, PLC and partnership forms. It excludes sole
traders and various other legal types. S011 has inconsistent sole-trader
wording; do not resolve that ambiguity by assumption.

Check actual activities and the current prohibited-sector list, operating
location, ownership and onboarding requirements before qualification.
Broad labels such as agency, e-commerce or software do not prove eligibility.

## Onboarding

S013 describes verification of identity, registration, registered and
operating addresses, business activities, controlling officers and owners.

The guide calls for ownership transparency for holders of 25% or greater.
Requirements can vary. No fixed approval time is established here.

Research records must not contain real identity documents or sensitive
prospect information.

## UK Pricing Applicability Resolution

S011 directly links S015, the consolidated current fee schedule.
Use S015 as the primary fee source for the UK-bank scenario.

It confirms:
- Basic: GBP 10 monthly.
- Grow: GBP 35 monthly or GBP 360 annually.
- Scale: GBP 125 monthly or GBP 1080 annually.

The documented transfer and FX allowances remain consistent. However,
S015 labels the domestic allowance as UK domestic payments. Do not carry
the earlier schedules' wider local-transfer classification into UK models.

S015 specifies Basic ATM withdrawal limits of GBP 3000 per 24 hours;
the marketing-page discrepancy remains recorded. ATM use stays outside
our initial comparison scenarios.

The Expenses app is GBP 8 per active team member monthly, with activity
defined by the schedule. Do not automatically charge every invited user.

Earlier S008-S010 observations remain in the audit trail but are secondary
to S015 for this scenario. The earlier pricing document's applicability
questions are resolved only to this stated extent.
