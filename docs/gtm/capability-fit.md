# Revolut Business — Capability-to-Need Assessment

**Research date:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** Analyst assessment; no customer validation conducted
**Evidence:** S006-S018 in sources.csv

## Candidate Fit

| Candidate | Hypothesised need | Relevant advertised capability | Qualification or gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Agencies with overseas clients | Receive foreign-currency revenue and reconcile receipts | Currency accounts, international payments and accounting integrations | Confirm payment routes and integration functions; exclude client-funds management |
| International e-commerce firms | Pay suppliers and understand currency-conversion costs | International transfers, FX allowances and team cards | Check goods, countries, payment methods and additional charges |
| Software and technology-service firms | Manage cross-border revenue and distributed expenditure | Currency accounts, cards, controls and integrations | Check actual activity, supported locations and required integrations |

This table links hypotheses to advertised capabilities. It does not prove
demand, savings, eligibility or superiority over competing providers.

## Plan-Level Implications

S007's comparison lists custom approvals, account permissions and the
Business API on Grow and higher plans, rather than Basic. Treat these as
plan-dependent capabilities and recheck before final publication.

The simulation's JWT, tenant isolation and monitoring implementation
does not demonstrate the provider's production implementation.

## Commercial Qualification

Before recommending a plan, establish:

1. Legal form, actual business activities and relevant locations.
2. Whether funds are earned business revenue or money held for clients.
3. Monthly transfer counts, routes, currencies and FX values.
4. Required users, approval controls and accounting integrations.
5. Existing total charges and operational dependencies.
6. Preferred billing commitment and tolerance for additional fees.

## Claims We Can and Cannot Make

Supported:
- Official pages advertise relevant payment and administrative features.
- Published fee schedules permit explicit usage-cost scenarios.
- Account and sector eligibility require individual checks.

Not established:
- Guaranteed savings or faster onboarding for the target segments.
- Verified customer willingness to switch or pay.
- Exact hours saved by integrations or automated controls.
- A quantified eligible or reachable market for any candidate.
- That any one candidate is the best target before competitor and
  segment-scoring work.

## Working Conclusion

All three candidates remain under consideration. Cross-border activity
and operational fit should drive qualification, rather than employee
count alone. Select the primary segment only after competitor analysis
and explicit segment scoring.
