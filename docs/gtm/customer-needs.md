# Customer Needs and Buying Triggers

## Verified Context

S004 reports export participation of 18%, direct import participation
of 24%, and technology/web-based software use of 74% among UK SME
employers. The survey covers 1-249 employees.

Do not add import and export percentages: businesses may do both.
Do not multiply these rates by the 1-49 population baseline.
Technology adoption does not establish demand for a particular provider.

## Commercial Hypotheses to Validate

| Candidate segment | Possible banking need | Possible buying trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Agencies with overseas clients | Understand receipt costs, FX charges and payment reconciliation | First overseas contract or increasing foreign-currency receipts |
| International e-commerce businesses | Compare supplier-payment costs and manage multiple currency flows | New overseas supplier or rising international payment volume |
| Software and technology-service firms | Reconcile international receipts and control team expenditure | Overseas customer growth or additional spending teams |

These are analyst hypotheses, not survey findings, verified product
benefits or customer interview results.

## Discovery Questions

1. Which currencies and countries are involved in monthly payments?
2. What are the payment volumes, values and existing total charges?
3. Where does reconciliation require manual work?
4. Which controls and integrations are essential?
5. What event would justify evaluating another provider?
6. What would prevent switching or adding a secondary account?

## Evidence Gaps

- Segment-specific prevalence and intensity of these needs.
- Current satisfaction, switching intent and willingness to pay.
- Provider eligibility and suitability for each business type.
- Verifiable cost comparisons using realistic usage scenarios.
