# Objections and Evidence-Based Responses

**Research date:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** Hypothetical discovery material; not customer-tested
**Audience:** Provisional agency ICP

## Response Framework

Acknowledge the concern, establish the requirement, check the evidence
and agree whether further evaluation is worthwhile.

| Possible objection | Proposed response | Evidence or next action |
|---|---|---|
| Our current bank works well | What, if anything, would justify reviewing it? If there is no meaningful problem, there may be no reason to change. | Establish a specific need before proposing an alternative |
| Another account has no monthly fee | Compare the complete cost for your usage and the functions you actually require. Our domestic scenario does not support a cheapest-provider claim for Revolut. | Use the pricing scenarios; resolve missing charges |
| Switching would disrupt operations | Map existing dependencies first. Compare a secondary account and a full switch separately, including added administration. | Document payment dependencies and migration effort |
| Will this work with our accounting software? | A named integration is only the starting point. Check your software plan, currencies and required functions. | Verify the exact workflow before recommending adoption |
| Are our funds protected? | Establish the contractual entity, product and applicable protection arrangements for the specific account. | Consult current official terms and relevant regulator or scheme information |
| Can you guarantee approval or fast onboarding? | Approval and timing depend on provider checks and the applicant. No guarantee is offered. | Check current eligibility and document requirements |
| We need cash, cheques or in-person support | Those requirements must be included in provider selection. Our current cost scenarios exclude them. | Expand the comparison or retain a better-fitting alternative |
| We manage money for clients | Client-money management is outside this pilot's scope. Do not assume an ordinary business account supports it. | Stop this pilot qualification and refer to appropriate specialist advice |
| We are not ready to evaluate | Establish whether there is a future trigger. Respect a decision not to continue. | Record the outcome without inventing an opportunity |

## Handling Unknowns

- Say when evidence is missing.
- Record the question, source needed and person responsible.
- Do not replace an unresolved fact with a confident sales claim.
- Correct earlier statements if new evidence changes the conclusion.
- Keep provider approval decisions separate from internal qualification.
