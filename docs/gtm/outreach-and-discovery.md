## Proposed Discovery Agenda: 20 Minutes

- 2 minutes: explain the independent research and confirm participation.
- 5 minutes: understand the business and payment workflow.
- 5 minutes: explore problems, frequency and impact.
- 5 minutes: establish requirements and decision criteria.
- 3 minutes: summarise findings and agree any voluntary next step.

## Discovery Questions

1. How do you currently receive overseas revenue and pay contractors?
2. Which currencies, countries and payment methods are involved?
3. What works well with the current arrangement?
4. Where, if anywhere, do costs or manual tasks cause difficulty?
5. How often does that happen, and how do you assess its impact?
6. Which accounting connections and approval controls are essential?
7. What banking services would you need to retain?
8. Who would evaluate and approve a different arrangement?
9. What would justify a review, and what would prevent one?
10. What have we missed or misunderstood?

Ask about current experience before introducing a provider.
Accept answers that contradict the proposed positioning.
