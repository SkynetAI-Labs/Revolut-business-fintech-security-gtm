# Pricing Comparison Scenarios

**Research date:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** Hypothetical usage assumptions; provider calculations pending

## Common Basis

- Applicant: UK limited company, subject to provider approval.
- Currency for reported costs: GBP.
- Period: one month of usage.
- Compare monthly billing first; show annual commitments separately.
- No promotional discounts assumed.
- No cash, cheques, borrowing, acquiring or ATM withdrawals.
- No paid expense-management add-ons in the initial comparison.
- State onboarding and other one-off fees separately.
- Do not treat unverified charges as zero.

## Usage Scenarios

| Input | A: Domestic baseline | B: Cross-border agency |
|---|---|---|
| Users | One director | One director |
| Outgoing UK GBP electronic transfers | 20 | 20 |
| Incoming UK GBP electronic transfers | 5 | 5 |
| Foreign-currency receipts | None | One EUR receipt of EUR 6,000 |
| Currency conversion | None | Convert EUR 6,000 to GBP once |
| International outgoing transfers | None | One EUR 1,000 payment to a German supplier |
| Timing | Weekday, standard processing | Weekday during applicable FX market hours |
| Starting funds | Sufficient GBP balance | Sufficient separate EUR balance for supplier payment |

Scenario B converts the full EUR 6,000 receipt. The EUR 1,000 supplier
payment uses a separate existing EUR balance and must not be deducted
again from the conversion amount.

These scenarios are comparison tools. They do not establish typical
segment behaviour or customer demand.

## Calculation Structure

Report each component separately:

1. Account subscription.
2. Domestic transaction charges.
3. Receipt charges.
4. Currency-conversion fees and exchange-rate basis.
5. International transfer charges.
6. Required optional services.
7. One-off setup costs.

Show first-month and recurring-month costs separately where relevant.

## Quote Controls

For Scenario B, record the quote timestamp, payment rail, funding method,
recipient amount and exchange rate. Compare the same route and timing.

If a provider cannot support the specified flow, mark it unsupported
or unresolved with evidence. Do not substitute a different product
without identifying it as a separate comparison.
