## Deposit Protection

S017 describes protection for eligible business deposits up to GBP 120000.
A separate legal entity is the protected business, not each director.
Accounts under the same relevant authorisation share the applicable limit.
Exclusions apply, including to authorised financial services firms.

Combine this general guidance with the actual account agreement and
deposit information sheet. Do not assume all Revolut-branded balances,
e-money accounts or investment products receive identical protection.
Protection against bank failure is not protection against every loss.

## Candidate-Segment Screening

- Agencies: distinguish earned service revenue from money held for clients.
  Investigate advertising budgets, escrow and payment pass-through models.
- E-commerce: check actual goods, sales model and restricted activities.
  Do not assume every online retailer is supported.
- Software/services: check the underlying activity, including financial
  services, gambling, streaming or other restricted business models.

These are qualification checks, not findings that the candidate segments
are generally prohibited.

S018 prohibits managing money that still belongs to clients. Receiving
payment for the business's own supplied services is a different situation;
ambiguous arrangements require provider clarification.
