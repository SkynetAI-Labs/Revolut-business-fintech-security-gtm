# Buyer-Specific Messaging

**Research date:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** Hypothetical messaging for an independent portfolio case study
**Audience:** Provisional UK limited-company agency ICP
**Use:** Draft concepts only; no messages sent or tested

## Founder or Managing Director

Message:
As overseas business grows, check whether your account arrangement
still fits your payment costs and operating needs. Compare Revolut
Business with relevant alternatives using your actual requirements.

Discovery question:
What would make reviewing your current arrangement worthwhile?

Evidence required:
A meaningful business problem, complete usage costs and the buyer's
decision criteria.

## Finance Lead or Bookkeeper

Message:
Map how overseas receipts, conversion and contractor payments reach
your accounts. Then check whether the available accounting connection
supports that workflow and what the complete arrangement costs.

Discovery question:
Where do reconciliation exceptions or manual tasks occur today?

Evidence required:
Current workflow, required integration functions and measured effort.
Do not promise a reduction in administration before testing.

## Operations Lead

Message:
Check who needs to prepare, approve and monitor payments, then compare
those requirements with the permissions available on the relevant plan.

Discovery question:
Which access or approval requirements must a new arrangement support?

Evidence required:
Named responsibilities, required permissions, plan availability and
implementation effort.

## External Accountant

Message:
Review the proposed account and accounting connection against the
agency's reporting and reconciliation requirements before any change.

Discovery question:
Which data, currencies and reconciliation functions are essential?

Evidence required:
Supported integration scope, software subscription requirements and
any remaining manual work.

## Message Consistency

Every version should:

- Begin with a problem to investigate.
- Keep costs and operational trade-offs visible.
- Present alternatives fairly.
- Avoid guaranteed savings, approval or payment timing.
- Identify SkynetAI-Labs as independent if used externally.
- Avoid implying an authorised Revolut sales relationship.

## Proposed Message Evaluation

If a future research exercise is authorised, record whether each
message is understood, relevant and credible. Capture objections and
lack of interest as well as positive responses.

No response rates, qualified leads or customer preferences have been
measured. Do not describe these drafts as proven messaging.
