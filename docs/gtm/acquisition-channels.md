# Proposed Acquisition Channels

**Research date:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** Hypothetical pilot design; no campaigns conducted
**Audience:** Provisional UK limited-company agency ICP

## Objective

Identify channels that could produce relevant discovery conversations
and, where justified, qualified product evaluations.

Channel priority is an analyst assumption. No response rates,
partnerships, customer acquisition costs or conversions are established.

## Initial Channel Priorities

| Channel | Proposed activity | Reason to test | Main uncertainty |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder-led LinkedIn content | Publish educational payment-cost and workflow comparisons if separately authorised | Explore whether relevant agency decision-makers engage | Audience access and qualified interest are unvalidated |
| Agency communities | Participate with moderator permission and invite voluntary research discussions | Learn how agencies describe payment and administration needs | Community access and demand are unknown |
| Accountant and bookkeeper introductions | Explore voluntary introductions if separately authorised | Investigate whether advisers can identify relevant operational needs | No adviser relationships or referral arrangements exist |

These channels are proposed tests, not proven acquisition routes.

## Channel Measurement

Track separately for each channel:

- Direct expenditure and staff time.
- Relevant enquiries or introductions.
- Discovery conversations attended.
- Conversations meeting the qualification criteria.
- Product evaluations voluntarily started.
- Reasons for declining or failing qualification.

Define each funnel stage consistently before recording results.
Engagement such as views and likes is not equivalent to qualified demand.

## Attribution

Record one originating channel for each unique business.
Record later interactions separately as assisted touchpoints.
Do not count multiple contacts from the same business as multiple
acquired customers.

Use only information necessary for an authorised research exercise.
No prospect list or personal information is collected by this document.

## Paid Advertising

Do not include paid advertising in the initial channel test.
Reconsider only after evidence supports the message, qualification
process and plausible acquisition economics.

This is a proposed sequencing decision, not evidence that advertising
would be ineffective.

## Decision Rules

- Continue a channel only when it produces relevant learning or
  qualified interest at an acceptable measured cost.
- Revise the message when engagement does not translate into fit.
- Reconsider the channel or ICP when access proves impractical.
- Define numerical thresholds alongside the commercial model before
  a real pilot; do not invent successful results.
