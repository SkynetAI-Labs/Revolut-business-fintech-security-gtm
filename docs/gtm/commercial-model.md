# Illustrative Commercial Model

**Prepared:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** Hypothetical assumptions; no campaign results
**Horizon:** 90-day pilot, with a separate illustrative annual revenue view

## 1. Funnel Assumptions

The funnel begins with unique businesses voluntarily making a relevant
enquiry. It does not begin with impressions or the entire UK SME market.

| Assumption | Downside | Base | Upside |
|---|---|---|---|
| Relevant enquiries | 20 | 40 | 60 |
| Enquiry to attended discovery | 40% | 50% | 60% |
| Attended discovery to qualified | 40% | 50% | 60% |
| Qualified to evaluation started | 25% | 40% | 50% |
| Evaluation to activation | 40% | 50% | 60% |

Activation includes provider approval and an agreed qualifying use.
The final rate combines application, approval and first use for modelling
only. A real pilot must track those stages separately.

These inputs are assumptions, not industry benchmarks or evidence that
the selected channels can generate the enquiry volumes.

## 4. Illustrative Revenue

As a limited illustration, assume every expected activation chooses
Grow on monthly billing at GBP35 per month,
using S015's observed price. This is not a plan recommendation or a
forecast of customer plan selection.

Base expected activations: 2.00.
Illustrative annual gross subscription billings:
2.00 x GBP35 x 12
= GBP840.00.

This annual view assumes all modelled accounts are active for a full
12 months, remain on the same price and do not churn. It is not revenue
earned during the 90-day pilot.

Gross subscription billings are not profit or contribution. No payment
revenue, servicing costs, incentives, losses, taxes or retention estimates
are established. Recheck pricing before publication or execution.

SkynetAI-Labs has no assumed commission or referral agreement.
Modelled cash income to SkynetAI-Labs is GBP0. The project is an
educational portfolio exercise, not a revenue-generating partnership.

## 5. Sensitivity and Break-Even

The scenario table varies enquiry volume and conversion assumptions
while holding resources constant. This assumes the same resources can
support each workload and must be checked before execution.

Provider profitability cannot be established without contribution,
retention and provider cost data.

A purely illustrative break-even formula is:

Required activations =
total economic pilot cost / contribution per activation.

At an ASSUMED GBP300 contribution per activation over a defined
12-month horizon, GBP2,400 / GBP300 = 8 activations.

GBP300 is not a verified Revolut figure. It demonstrates the formula,
not commercial viability. Gross subscription billings must not be
substituted for contribution.

## 6. Proposed Decision Thresholds

These thresholds are analyst-selected management rules, not benchmarks.

- Day 30: seek at least 5 attended discoveries, with at least 3 businesses
  independently describing a relevant recurring problem.
- Day 60: seek at least 5 qualified businesses and 2 explicit evaluation
  decisions; review all disqualification reasons.
- Day 90: compare observed results with the base planning case of
  40 enquiries, 20 attended discoveries, 10 qualified businesses,
  4 evaluations and 2 activations.
- Remain within the proposed GBP150 cash and 75-hour resource limits,
  or obtain a revised execution decision before exceeding them.
- Missing a threshold triggers review or revision, not automatic scaling.
- Meeting a threshold does not establish profitability or causality.
- Do not scale while material cost, eligibility or functionality gaps
  prevent a fair customer comparison.

## 7. Measurement Rules

Record channel, unique business reference, stage dates, direct costs,
staff time and known outcomes. Minimise personal data.

Report conversion numerators and denominators, pending outcomes and
observation periods. Separate model assumptions from actual results.

The base expected activation count is small. Any future pilot result
would provide limited evidence and should not be extrapolated directly
to the national market.

## Conclusion

The model makes resource and conversion assumptions explicit.
It does not establish demand, provider profitability or a viable
commercial arrangement for SkynetAI-Labs.
