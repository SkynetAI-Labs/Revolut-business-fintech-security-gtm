# Provisional Ideal Customer Profile

**Research date:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** Analyst-defined pilot hypothesis; not customer-validated
**Basis:** target-segments.md and segment-scoring.md

## Selected Segment

UK limited companies employing 1-49 people that provide marketing,
design or similar agency services and receive recurring overseas
payments for their own work.

Agencies are the provisional focus for a hypothetical 90-day pilot.
Their scoring advantage depends on unvalidated buyer-access and
pilot-simplicity assumptions. Technology services remain an alternative.

## Positive Qualification Signals

- Recurring foreign-currency invoices or overseas contractor payments.
- A specific payment-cost or administration problem worth investigating.
- Access to the person responsible for banking and payment decisions.
- Willingness to compare current costs using actual payment routes.
- Required integrations and controls that can be checked against
  the applicable provider plan.
- A defined reason to evaluate now, such as a new overseas contract.

These are proposed discovery criteria, not verified segment attributes.
No minimum revenue or payment value is asserted without validation.

## Exclusions from the Proposed Pilot

- Sole traders, partnerships and other legal forms outside our
  chosen limited-company research boundary.
- Client-money holding, escrow or management of client-owned budgets.
- Unsupported activities, countries or ownership arrangements.
- Businesses requiring services whose suitability remains unresolved.
- Prospects seeking guaranteed savings, approval or payment timing.
- Businesses without a relevant problem or willingness to evaluate.

These are pilot boundaries, not a complete statement of any provider's
eligibility rules. The provider makes the account approval decision.

## Use Case to Explore

Receiving earned revenue from overseas clients, understanding the
cost of converting it, paying overseas contractors from business
funds and reconciling those transactions.

Compare a secondary account with a full switch separately. Establish
existing banking dependencies before proposing either approach.

## What Would Challenge This Selection?

- Buyers cannot be reached economically.
- Discovery finds little material payment or administration friction.
- Required functions or eligibility do not fit.
- Complete usage costs do not justify the proposed benefits.
- Technology-service firms show stronger validated demand or access.

## Validation Gate

Before a real acquisition pilot, gather permissioned discovery evidence,
check representative payment costs and confirm operational requirements.
Record findings that contradict the ICP and revise the selection.

No outreach, applications, expenditure or customer commitments have
been undertaken through this portfolio exercise.
