# Buyer Roles and Qualification

**Research date:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** Discovery hypotheses; no buyer interviews conducted
**Target:** Provisional agency ICP in ideal-customer-profile.md

## Buyer Roles

| Role hypothesis | Possible responsibility | Question to establish involvement |
|---|---|---|
| Founder or managing director | Business priorities, budget and final approval | Who approves changes to banking providers and subscriptions? |
| Finance lead or bookkeeper | Payments, reconciliation and cost visibility | Who manages payment operations and accounting connections? |
| Operations lead | Contractor payments and internal processes | Who would implement and maintain the proposed workflow? |
| External accountant | Advice and accounting requirements | Does an accountant influence provider or integration selection? |

One person may perform several roles. Do not assume job title establishes
decision authority, account access or willingness to buy.

## Pain and Trigger Hypotheses

| Possible pain | Evidence to request in discovery | Possible trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Unclear international payment costs | An anonymised breakdown of fees, exchange rates and routes | Increasing overseas invoice volume |
| Manual reconciliation | A description of the workflow and measured effort | More transactions or a finance-system change |
| Limited visibility or permissions | Required user roles and current approval process | Hiring staff or adding payment responsibility |
| Operational complexity across accounts | Current account dependencies and reconciliation tasks | Reviewing the existing banking arrangement |

These are questions to investigate, not established customer problems.
Do not request credentials, identity documents or identifiable bank
statements for this educational exercise.

## Qualification Questions

1. Is the business within our legal-form and activity boundary?
2. Are funds earned business revenue rather than money held for clients?
3. Which countries, currencies, payment rails and monthly volumes apply?
4. What problem occurs, how often, and what is its measurable impact?
5. What are the current subscription, transaction and conversion costs?
6. Which integrations, controls and service channels are essential?
7. Who evaluates, approves and implements a provider change?
8. What would justify evaluation now, and what would prevent adoption?

## Proposed Qualification Outcomes

- Proceed to comparison: relevant problem, appropriate decision-maker,
  sufficient usage information and no identified scope conflict.
- Further discovery: material requirements or costs remain unknown.
- Outside pilot scope: activity, legal form or operational requirements
  conflict with the ICP boundaries.
- No current opportunity: no meaningful problem or evaluation interest.

These are internal research outcomes. They are not provider account
eligibility decisions or predictions of approval.

## Handoff to Positioning

Use the buyer's verified problem and requirements to shape the message.
Keep payment-cost claims conditional on a complete comparison and keep
operational benefits conditional on the required functionality.

No qualified prospects, pipeline value or customer validation are
claimed by this document.
