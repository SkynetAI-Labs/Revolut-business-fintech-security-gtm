# Lab 5 — Evidence Standards

## Claim Types

- Verified fact: directly supported by an identified source.
- Analysis: an interpretation of evidence, with its reasoning stated.
- Assumption: an unverified input used for planning or modelling.

## Source Selection

- Prefer UK government and official statistical publications for market data.
- Use official provider pages and terms for features, pricing and eligibility.
- Use regulator sources for regulatory status and protection arrangements.
- Treat provider marketing claims as attributed claims, not independent proof.
- Use secondary research only with its methodology and limitations recorded.

## Recording Requirements

- Assign each source a stable identifier, starting with S001.
- Record the exact URL, publisher, title and access date.
- Record publication date and reporting period separately when available.
- Do not treat the access date as the publication date.
- Record the supported claim, relevant section and limitations.
- Distinguish planned sources from sources actually accessed.
- Identify the relevant country, currency, plan and billing basis.
- Do not infer missing information or use an unresolved source as evidence.

## Comparisons and Models

- Compare equivalent usage scenarios and disclose exclusions.
- Record allowances, additional fees, taxes and promotional conditions
  where applicable.
- Keep model assumptions separate from verified pricing.
- Never present hypothetical conversions, revenue or customer feedback
  as measured results.

## Research Integrity

- Paraphrase findings and retain attribution.
- Record conflicting evidence and unresolved questions.
- Recheck material pricing, eligibility and product claims before publication.
