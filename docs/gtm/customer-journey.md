# Proposed Customer Journey

**Research date:** 23 October 2026
**Status:** Hypothetical design; no prospects or customers acquired
**Audience:** Provisional UK limited-company agency ICP

## Journey and Exit Criteria

| Stage | Proposed activity | Evidence required to advance |
|---|---|---|
| Awareness | Encounter independent educational content or an introduction | Business voluntarily expresses relevant interest |
| Discovery | Discuss payment activity, problems and existing arrangements | Attended conversation with sufficient information to assess fit |
| Qualification | Check ICP boundaries, requirements and decision process | Relevant problem, appropriate decision-maker and no identified scope conflict |
| Comparison | Assess equivalent usage costs and operational requirements | Buyer reviews documented alternatives, costs and unresolved gaps |
| Evaluation | Buyer voluntarily decides whether to investigate a provider | Explicit evaluation decision; no account approval implied |
| Application | Buyer applies directly through the provider, if desired | Application submission confirmed voluntarily by the buyer |
| Activation | Approved account supports a defined first use | Approval and qualifying use confirmed; do not infer from submission |
| Follow-up | Assess operational fit and continuing use | Voluntary feedback and evidence against agreed success criteria |

Provider application and account decisions are outside SkynetAI-Labs'
control. No referral relationship, application access or tracking
integration is assumed.

## Funnel Definitions

- Enquiry: one unique business expressing relevant interest.
- Discovery attended: an actual completed discovery conversation.
- Qualified opportunity: a business meeting the documented internal
  qualification criteria; not a provider eligibility decision.
- Evaluation started: an explicit decision to investigate a provider.
- Application submitted: distinct from approval.
- Activated account: provider approval plus an agreed qualifying use;
  an illustrative future metric, not a measured result.

Record unknown outcomes as unknown. Do not count an evaluation,
application or approval as an activated customer.

## Handoffs

Record the originating channel, current stage, next agreed action,
responsible person and date, if a real exercise is authorised.

A request for information is not permission to apply on someone's behalf.
Direct applicants to official provider channels. Do not collect account
credentials, identity documents or sensitive banking records.

## Exit and Pause Outcomes

- No relevant need: close the opportunity without inventing demand.
- Outside pilot scope: record the qualification reason.
- Missing evidence: pause comparison until material gaps are resolved.
- Alternative fits better: record that conclusion fairly.
- Buyer declines: respect the decision and stop follow-up.
- Provider declines: keep separate from internal qualification failure.

## Measurement Limits

Views, enquiries, qualified opportunities and activated accounts are
different measures. Track each stage separately and avoid double counting.

Any future conversion rates must state the denominator, observation
period and treatment of pending outcomes. No conversion rates or
customer outcomes have been measured in this portfolio exercise.
