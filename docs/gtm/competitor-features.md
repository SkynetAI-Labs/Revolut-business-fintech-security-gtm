# Competitor Feature Comparison

**Research date:** 23 October 2026
**Evidence:** S006-S007, S015 and S019-S021
**Status:** Provider-advertised features; analyst interpretation

## Initial Comparison

| Area | Revolut Business | Wise Business | Starling Business | HSBC Small Business Banking |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| International activity | Advertises currency accounts, FX and international transfers | Advertises foreign-currency receipts and international payments | Advertises international transfers; check supported destinations | Verify international services and charges separately |
| Everyday administration | Advertises cards, expense tools and accounting integrations | Advertises team cards, team finance tools and accounting connections | Advertises notifications, receipt attachments, Spaces and spending analytics | Advertises digital payments and connected-account visibility |
| Access and service | Verify required service channels | Advertises phone, email and chat support | App and online banking; Post Office cash deposits subject to charges | Advertises digital access and in-person or virtual Business Specialists |
| Important qualification | Some controls and API access depend on plan | Verify route costs, permissions and contractual arrangements | Multiple-director access does not establish equivalent employee permissions | Sage-linked offer described for eligible sole traders must not be assumed available to our limited-company scenario |

## Interpretation

- Cross-border businesses should compare actual receipt, conversion and
  payment routes before choosing a provider.
- Domestic banking and physical service requirements may change the
  shortlist independently of international-payment needs.
- Similar feature labels do not establish equivalent functionality,
  permissions, integrations or service quality.
- A feature not established here is an evidence gap, not proof that the
  provider lacks it.

have been conducted. This comparison does not establish savings,
