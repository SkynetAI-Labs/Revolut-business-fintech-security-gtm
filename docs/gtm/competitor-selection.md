## Selection Rationale

The shortlist represents different alternatives a UK limited company
might evaluate. Selection does not establish market share, superiority
or eligibility for a particular applicant.

It is intentionally limited for a portfolio case study. Other providers
and combinations of providers may be relevant.

## Comparison Rules

- Use UK business products, not personal accounts.
- Compare a clearly defined legal form and usage scenario.
- Verify contractual entity and protection model separately.
- Do not describe all providers as equivalent banks or assume identical
  deposit protection.
- Separate subscription, transaction, FX and optional-feature costs.
- Treat a secondary-account combination as a distinct scenario.
- Do not infer a missing feature from its absence on a marketing page.
- Keep promotional and annual-billing terms distinct from standard pricing.
